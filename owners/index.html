An offering for the owners behind ConnectPay's smallest businesses: four to six people, often with family on the team, and an owner who is the last person to get any attention.

How we'll work

Four commitments that shape everything else on this page.

No salespeople at the table

No sales calls and no pitch. An owner answers a few questions and talks with an advisor only when they choose to.

Clear scope

What the advisor covers and what goes back to ConnectPay is written down before the first owner is invited.

Your partners come first

When an owner's plan calls for something a ConnectPay partner provides, that partner gets the first call.

Your relationship

ConnectPay chooses the owners, the timing and the message. AssetAlign does the work behind it.

What the advisor covers

  • One plan for the owner and the household, with the business in the picture
  • Paying yourself when income is uneven
  • Saving for the owner's own retirement and how each option is taxed
  • What happens to the owner's income and the business if they can't work
  • Life after the business: passing it on, selling it or winding it down
  • A will, beneficiaries and who can act if the owner can't
  • Bringing a spouse or family member into the plan

What goes to ConnectPay's partners first

  • Coverage for the business itself
  • Employee benefits and group health coverage
  • Setting up or changing a retirement plan for the company
  • Payroll, HR and tax filing for the business

A good plan will surface these needs. ConnectPay tells us who handles each one, and the owner is pointed there.

2

Who it's for

At this size the owner and the business are the same thing. That is what makes this offering different from anything built for employees.

4 to 6 people

A shop, a restaurant, a small practice. The owner opens, closes, covers shifts and signs the checks.

Family on the team

A spouse does the books or a son or daughter works the floor. The household depends on one business.

Everything in one place

Most of what the owner has built is inside the business, and most of it depends on the owner showing up.

3

Different questions, for a different reason

Our employee questions exist to work out which services fit each person. An owner doesn't need to be sorted: every owner is a fit for an advisor. So the questions have one job, which is to let the advisor walk in already understanding this owner. We don't ask about income or balances at all.
