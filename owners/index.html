How we'll work

Four commitments to ConnectPay.

Introduced by email

Owners hear about it in an email from ConnectPay that leads to a landing page. It asks nothing of your sales team.

Clear scope

What the advisor covers and what goes back to ConnectPay is written down before the first owner is invited.

Your partners come first

When an owner's plan calls for something a ConnectPay partner provides, that partner gets the first call.

Your voice

We don't speak for ConnectPay. You choose the owners and the timing, and you approve every word they see.

What the advisor covers

  • One plan for the owner and the household, with the business in the picture
  • Profit: paying yourself, what you keep, and how retirement options are taxed
  • Protection: the owner's income, family and ownership if they can't work
  • Personal Freedom: passing the business on, selling it or winding it down
  • A will, beneficiaries and who can act if the owner can't

What goes to ConnectPay's partners first

  • Coverage for the business itself
  • Employee benefits and group health coverage
  • Setting up or changing a retirement plan for the company
  • Payroll, HR and tax filing for the business

A good plan will surface these needs. ConnectPay tells us who handles each one, and the owner is pointed there.
