AssetAlign Main Street

Big-company financial benefits, built for small businesses.

A version of AssetAlign designed for ConnectPay's small-business clients and their employees: plain language, no corporate assumptions, a personalized plan for every employee at no cost to them, and a way to invite family.

We call this program AssetAlign Main Street. It's the working name for ConnectPay and AssetAlign. Employees simply see AssetAlign, made available by their employer.

Product language and offering

The core stays the same: a personalized financial plan from a vetted, independent advisor, rewards for taking action, and help with decisions as life changes. What changes is how we ask, what we assume and how we talk.

A dedicated advisor group for Main Street

We'll put together a smaller group from our advisor network who specifically work with people at smaller companies. Every Main Street employee is matched with one of them, so the advisor already understands their situation before the first meeting.

The same vetting standards as our full network, with experience that fits how small-business employees actually live and save.

What these advisors know well

  • Retirement plans beyond the traditional 401(k), including SIMPLE IRAs, SEP IRAs and state retirement programs
  • Starting retirement savings from scratch when there's no plan at work
  • Hourly and variable pay, overtime and tips
  • Side income and the taxes that come with it
  • Planning around health coverage from a spouse or the marketplace
  • Building an emergency cushion before investing

5

Rollout approach

ConnectPay owns the client relationship and decides how and when its clients hear about the program. AssetAlign does the work behind it, so it adds as little as possible to your team's day.

1

An introduction, where it fits

ConnectPay decides which clients hear about the program, when and how. We'll provide a short summary to make it easy, and if a conversation with the owner would help, we're glad to join one alongside ConnectPay.

2

Employees get invited

A co-branded email and the short video. No company codes or forms to dig up, so getting started takes about two minutes.

3

It keeps working

Timely nudges at life moments and advisor follow-ups keep employees moving, with no work for the owner.

What AssetAlign builds and runs for the pilot

  • The Main Street onboarding. New and reworded questions, the savings slider and retirement options beyond a 401(k).
  • Plain-language copy everywhere. Every screen, email and reminder rewritten for small businesses.
  • No company codes. Every employee comes in through ConnectPay, so there is no code to enter at sign-up.
  • Co-branded launch materials. The employee email and a ConnectPay version of the launch video.
  • A dedicated Main Street advisor group. Advisors who specialize in people at smaller companies, matched to every employee.
  • Reminders that bring people back. Timely nudges that keep employees moving after they sign up.
  • Member support. Help for every employee in the pilot, from first login to their plan meeting.
  • ConnectPay's ideaInvite a family member. Employees can invite a spouse, partner or family member to get a plan of their own.

Pilot cohorts

We suggest starting with a pilot group of client businesses selected by ConnectPay, run as one group or as a few cohorts.

50 to 100

Client businesses in the pilot, enough to see clear results across different kinds of teams.

1,000 to 2,000

Employees, roughly, depending on which businesses are chosen.

Your call

ConnectPay picks the businesses and how to group them, for example by employee size, entirely at its discretion.

How we'd measure it

  • Share of employees who start and finish onboarding
  • Financial plans completed and advisor meetings booked
  • Actions taken and Align Credits earned
  • Employee satisfaction, and owner feedback on the program
